To those who cannot be calmed

You already know the shape of it. The film ends, the credits roll, and something inside you keeps running — walking the same corridor, opening the same door, waiting for a decision that never comes.

Most platforms are built for people who want to be soothed. A recommendation, a sequel, a franchise, a two-hour nap with explosions. PSYCHOTIC is built for the others: those who want the image to stay behind the eyes long after the screen goes black, who would rather be disturbed than entertained, and who consider a seven-hour Hungarian film in the rain a reasonable way to spend a Tuesday.

Here you will find horror that refuses to explain itself, chaos with a structure underneath, and stress engineered on purpose. Films that ask something of you. Some of them will be interactive and will demand that you choose. Some will simply sit there, heavy and slow and unbearable, until you break first.

There is no algorithm here trying to guess what you already like. There is a catalogue, a player, and a long corridor. The rest is yours.

Welcome. Make yourself uncomfortable.
